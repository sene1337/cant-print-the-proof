Money tells its own story: shell, stone, gold, paper, proof. Paper can be printed without limit. Proof has to be earned, and there will only ever be twenty-one million.

Every frame is a block

The film runs at 30 frames a second, and each frame is its own proof-of-work block: an 80-byte header that points to the block before it, commits to the SHA-256 fingerprint of the song, and carries a nonce that took real computing work to find. The first block points to Bitcoin's own genesis block. The strip along the bottom of the film shows the block you are watching; its leading zeros are the work.

It is an art chain, not Bitcoin, and it earns nothing. It shows the one thing a printer can't make: work you can check.

Made in the open

    
There is no video file on this page. The film is about four thousand lines of JavaScript that draw each frame with three.js as the song plays, so the picture you see is being made on your own graphics chip. The same code, run frame by frame, renders the video file. The coins are photographed museum pieces, all public domain. The song was generated with ElevenLabs Music.
