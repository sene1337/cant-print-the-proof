A music video that is also a blockchain

Can't Print
the Proof

Plays live in your browser, drawn frame by frame. Sound on.

0:00

Every frame is a block

The video runs at 30 frames a second. Each frame is its own proof-of-work block: an 80-byte header that links to the previous block, commits to the SHA-256 of the song, and carries a nonce that took real work to find. The first block links to Bitcoin's real genesis block. Together that is 5,521 blocks and 1,444,915,378 hashes.

This is an art chain, not Bitcoin. It earns nothing. It shows the one thing a printer can't fake: work.
