The model: Bitcoin24 by Michael Saylor, Shirish Jajodia and Chaitanya Jain (open source). With the Bitcoin24 price model, Bitcoin starts at your price today in 2026 and then grows at the model's rate for each calendar year. In the Base case that rate is 50% in 2025 and falls 2.5 points a year to a floor of 20%. Bear runs from 25% to 18%, falling 0.5 points a year. Bull runs from 75% to 25%, falling 5 points a year.

Bitcoin24 stops at 2045. For later years the simulator uses its own extension, which is not Saylor's. Bitcoin grows only as fast as the world's other assets in the Bitcoin24 macro model, each asset class at its own growth rate, so Bitcoin's share of world assets stays at its 2045 level. With the power-law model, Bitcoin follows its long-run trend (price ≈ 1.0117×10⁻¹⁷ × days since the genesis block, raised to the 5.82), scaled by your price path. Trend price today works out to about , which can sit well above or below spot.

Your strategy acts today: it moves part of your shares, bonds and cash into Bitcoin, and Double and Triple Maxi also borrow against your real estate. Your other assets grow at Bitcoin24's rates, with bond and cash yields taxed. Your mortgage grows at its rate and is never paid down. Until your freedom year you invest your savings as your strategy says. From your freedom year you stop earning and buying. Your spending rises at your inflation rate and comes first from shares, bonds and cash, then from selling Bitcoin. Your real estate is never sold. What you own minus what you owe must stay above zero through 2090. Taxes on selling Bitcoin are not modelled; Bitcoin24 does not model them either. An illustration, not advice.
